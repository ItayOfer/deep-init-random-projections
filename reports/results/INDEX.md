Closed form derived and verified: per-layer forward gain `G(c) = √(1 − 2c/√π + c²)`, minimised at `c = 1/√π` where `G = r ≈ 0.8256` — **the same constant row-centering pays**, so exact DC removal and unit forward gain are incompatible. The brief's `√r` lead is **refuted**: the implied gain is depth-dependent (0.838 @L10 → 0.940 @L100 at `c = 1/√π`) and merely crosses `√r` near L=60; `c = 0.75` verifies `G` to 4 dp at every depth. The non-monotonicity in `c` is a **batch-statistic artifact** — with a per-sample RMS the theory is exact and `c = 1/√π` reaches cosine **0.0037 at 100 layers**. He is **47.6% dataset-dead at 100L** (N=512), every shift 0.000–0.100. `c = 0.1–0.25` beats both baselines on distance-correlation-to-input at 30L (0.691 vs He 0.388 on cifar10) |
